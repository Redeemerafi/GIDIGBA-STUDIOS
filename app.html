FAQ

How do I start?
Tap "Start a Project", pick what you need, tell us about your business and attach any product images you already have. We'll come back with a quote and timeline.
I only have phone pictures of my product. Is that enough?
Yes. Send what you have and tell us how you want the final content to look. We handle lighting, scenes and presentation.
Do you use AI for visuals and video?
Yes. AI is one of our production tools for commercials, product scenes and brand imagery. You always review the final work before delivery.
How much does it cost?
Every business is different. Send your brief and you'll get a clear quote before any payment.
How do I pay?
Mobile Money. Payment is confirmed before production starts.

You want Radikal Studios, our sister brand for personal photoshoots and individual visuals. We're here for businesses and brands.
