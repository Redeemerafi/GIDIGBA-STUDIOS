About

A studio built for how business actually runs.

GIDIGBA STUDIOS is a creative & technology studio in Accra. We help companies and individuals look and communicate at the level their work deserves — without the usual friction of traditional studios.

Who we serve

Companies, startups, product brands and organisations that need clear visual identity, content and campaigns. Individuals who want studio-quality photos without booking a physical session or waiting weeks.

The problem we solve

Traditional studio photography and agency processes are slow, expensive and geographically limited. We moved the entire photoshoot online and compressed delivery to 24 business hours, while keeping the full creative toolkit (branding, design, video, web, campaigns) under one roof.

How we work

Every conversion path ends on WhatsApp. There is no checkout form, no account system and no card gateway. Payment is Mobile Money or bank transfer. Photoshoots are 100% online — you never have to leave home or visit a physical studio.

Location & hours

Accra, Greater Accra, Ghana. Service area: Ghana (100% online). Business hours: Monday–Saturday, 08:00–20:00. WhatsApp: +233 53 431 7611.
